# Flowcoat content guide

Use direct Australian English. Say **powder coating of metal parts**, not floor coating, spray painting or “surface solutions”. Explain the customer’s next step and the information Flowcoat needs. Prefer process evidence over superlatives.

## Content controls

- Never publish a customer name, logo, project, drawing or testimonial without permission.
- Mark the new facility and automation as planned, never current.
- Do not claim maximum dimensions, lead times, warranties, certifications, environmental performance, collection or delivery coverage until approved in `CLAIMS_REGISTER.md`.
- No invented metrics, reviews, heritage, awards or “best in Sydney” language.
- Photography should show real work, safe practices, finish detail, full product context and protected handling.

## Project metadata checklist

Title; permission status; anonymous/client-approved status; category; material; colour; finish; quantity (approved only); custom/production; challenge; process; outcome; alt text; images; video; publication date.
